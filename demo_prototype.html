    How Falcon keeps the model up to date

    1. Camera flags low-confidence or missed objects in the field.
    2. Recreate that object/lighting/angle in the Falcon digital twin.
    3. Generate new labeled synthetic images automatically.
    4. Retrain YOLO, compare mAP@0.5 with the baseline, redeploy if better.
